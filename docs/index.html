Seamless 4-Step Setup Flow

Get your smart range extender up and running in under 60 seconds.

01
Connect ESP32 via USB
Plug your ESP32 board into your computer. Ensure you use a data-capable cable (not charge-only).
02
Select Version & Flash
Pick your desired version tab above, click Install, and choose your serial port in the browser prompt.
03
Automatic Deployment
The WebSerial flasher automatically flashes the production firmware at 921600 baud in ~25 seconds.
04
Connect & Roam
Join Wi-Fi AetherPulse-Extender (Pass: aetherpulse123) or browse to http://192.168.4.1 or http://aetherpulse.local.
