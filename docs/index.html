AETHERPULSE EXTREME

State-of-the-Art Wi-Fi Repeater & Range Extender for ESP32. Flash directly from your web browser in 30 seconds with zero drivers.

Latest Automated Build • v1.0.0

1-Click Web Serial Installer

Connect your ESP32 board to your computer via USB (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge required), then click the button below to flash the latest firmware.

⚠️ Web Serial is not supported in this browser. Please open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Desktop/Android.
Step 1
Connect Board
Plug your ESP32 board into USB. Ensure a data-capable cable is used.
Step 2
Click Install
Click 'Install' and select your board's COM/Serial port from the browser prompt.
Step 3
Connect & Enjoy
Connect to Wi-Fi AetherPulse-Extender (Pass: aetherpulse123) or browse to http://192.168.4.1.
